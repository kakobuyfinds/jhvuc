01

Begin with a wardrobe gap

Choose a purpose before a logo: everyday walking shoes, a midweight hoodie or a jacket that fits over knitwear. A clear use makes clothing material comparisons and outfit coordination easier.

02

Use a seller return policy check and an inspection photo checklist. Ask about unclear measurements, materials or defects while there is still time to change your selection.

03 / PikoBuy sizing guide

Measure the garment, then read the label.

Lay a well-fitting piece flat and compare like-for-like measurements. Confirm whether a seller reports flat width, full circumference or body measurements.

Category checks for fit and visible condition
Category Useful measurements Details to inspect
Shoes Foot length; available width information; insole measurement if supplied. For a sneaker size chart comparison, check the exact model. Review toe shape, heel symmetry and visible sole bonding.
Hoodies / Sweaters Chest width, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length. Hoodie chest measurement and sweater sleeve length help compare fit. Check cuffs, ribbing and stated fabric care.
T-Shirts / Jerseys Chest width, total length and sleeve opening. A T-shirt fit comparison should allow for the intended silhouette. For a jersey print inspection, look for distorted lettering and uneven seams.
Jackets / Sets Chest, shoulders, sleeves; separate top and bottom measurements for sets. Use a jacket layering fit check. Inspect zippers, lining and pocket construction; matching set sizing may differ between pieces.
Pants / Shorts Waist, rise, inseam, thigh and leg opening. For a pants inseam measurement, confirm the measuring points. A shorts waist comparison should account for elastic and drawstrings.
Underwear Waist range, rise and leg opening where available. Review underwear fabric composition, care instructions and hygiene-related return conditions before ordering.
Headwear / Accessories Hat circumference, scarf dimensions or jewelry length. Check adjustment hardware, stitching, edges and clasp function. Ask for material details if they are missing.

04 / Practical reading

Three notes to read before you order.

Simple habits make a spreadsheet more useful: compare evidence, write down costs and keep the product reference attached to every decision.

PikoBuy QC guide

Read the photo, not the promise.

Start a warehouse photo review by matching the item, color and size to your order. Then look at seam alignment, visible marks, shape and any measurements shown.

For a stitching quality check, request a closer view when the existing image is too distant. Lighting can change how colors appear. Inspection photos do not establish durability, comfort or authenticity.

PikoBuy shipping checklist

Give every cost its own line.

A total purchase cost estimate should separate the item price, local delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties.

For parcel weight planning, distinguish actual weight from volumetric weight. Packaging size, route and destination can affect a quote. Check the current service terms for your country before payment.

05 / Buying across borders

Plan for your destination.

Shopping advice becomes useful when it fits your location, delivery needs and ability to return an item.

For US, UK and European shoppers

Use your destination's current official customs information when researching import charges and restrictions. Do not assume that another buyer's parcel cost applies to your order.

For international delivery planning, check route availability, excluded goods, tracking and the service's stated handling process. Allow for uncertainty if you need an item for a specific event.

Keep measurements and currency clear

Check whether garment measurements are in centimeters or inches; 1 inch equals 2.54 centimeters. Record the original currency and confirm the payment provider's conversion and charges.

Before combining items into one parcel, check return eligibility and timing. Consolidated parcel planning should follow inspection and cost review, rather than replacing them.

How should I choose a clothing size?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check chest width, shoulder width, length and sleeves for tops, and waist, rise and inseam for trousers. Confirm the measurement method and units before choosing.

What should I check in QC photos?

Match the selected size, color and item to the order. Inspect seams, visible defects, shape, labels and any requested measurements. QC photos help identify visible issues but do not certify authenticity or long-term durability.

Does the item price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Confirm item cost, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Final charges depend on the seller, service, parcel and destination.
